### Fonts the owner likes

- **Cinzel**: Roman proportions, noble classical engraving style.
  - Source: Google Fonts, also on npm as `@fontsource-variable/cinzel`. Free for client work (SIL Open Font License 1.1). Variable weight 400–900.
  - Capitals only: there is no lowercase (lowercase letters come out as small capitals). Use it for headings, logos and short labels, never body text.
  - Keep it premium: wide letter-spacing (about 0.08–0.15em), large sizes, weights 400–600. Heavy weights and tight spacing make it look like a themed restaurant menu.
  - Pair with a quiet body font, for example Jost (modern contrast) or EB Garamond (fully classical).
  - Good fits: classic barbershop, salon, med spa, fine dining, law firm, luxury real estate.
  - Motion: suits slow, calm reveals (fade or mask, transform and opacity only), not bouncy springs.
  - Self-host it like every other font here: woff2 plus its OFL license file, loaded with `next/font/local`.
